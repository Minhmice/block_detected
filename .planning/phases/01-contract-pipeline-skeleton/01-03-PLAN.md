| T-01-09 | Repudiation | rejected/ambiguous statuses | medium | mitigate | Require non-empty `debug.rejection_reason` for candidate-forbidden rejection statuses so logs explain why no pickup geometry exists. |

| T-01-11 | Information Disclosure | debug fields | low | accept | Preserve only caller-provided string `frame_id` and deterministic method/reason strings; no frame object repr or pixel data. |
</threat_model>
